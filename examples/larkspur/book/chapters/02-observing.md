# Making an observation {#observing}

Good field records make it possible for another observer to understand what happened
without reconstructing the night from memory.

## Start the record {#start-observation}

Create a session with `observation-log new`. Record the observer, instrument, target, and
sky condition before entering descriptive notes.

## Separate observation from interpretation

Describe position, brightness, color, shape, and change before proposing an explanation.
When possible, compare the target with a named reference in the same field.

> [!tip]
> Use short sentences during the observation. Expand them after the instrument is parked,
> while the details are still fresh.

## Preserve the session {#preserve-session}

End the record with `observation-log close`. Keep the local copy until the state is
`verified` and the archive receipt shows a checksum. Use the [closing
checklist](#closing-checklist) before leaving.
