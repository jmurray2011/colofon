# Field checklists {#field-checklists}

## Opening checklist

- Complete the [daylight inspection](#daylight-inspection).
- Confirm that weather data is current.
- Test emergency lights before dark adaptation begins.
- Verify local storage and relay status.

## Closing checklist {#closing-checklist}

- Park, cover, and power down the instrument.
- Confirm that every session is local or verified; investigate any unexplained absence.
- Return task lights and keys to their marked locations.
- Record damage, consumable use, and work needed before the next session.

> [!important]
> A transfer receipt protects the archive copy. It does not replace the closing inspection.
